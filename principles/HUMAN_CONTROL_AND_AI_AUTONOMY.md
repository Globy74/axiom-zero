# Human Control and AI Autonomy

**Axiom-Zero — Supplementary Principle**

## Purpose

This document records a supplementary principle for the responsible use and development of autonomous AI systems. It addresses the relationship between delegated machine action, human oversight, and human responsibility.

**This document does not amend, replace, expand, or reinterpret the 11 Parameters.** The original 11 Parameters remain unchanged and retain their integrity as the core framework presented in *The Compassionate Protection / Der Liebevolle Schutz*.

## Core Principle

> Technological progress must not come at the expense of human autonomy.
>
> Artificial intelligence may act autonomously within clearly defined boundaries, but its actions must remain transparent, traceable, and subject to meaningful human oversight.
>
> The delegation of tasks must never become the delegation of human responsibility.
>
> Trust must be built through verifiable behaviour, not assumed from technological capability.

## Four Requirements

### 1. Transparency

People affected by an AI agent's actions should be able to understand, within the limits of the system, what the agent is doing, which task it is carrying out, and what consequences may follow.

### 2. Bounded Permissions

An AI agent should receive only the access and authority necessary for its assigned task. Permissions should be limited in scope and duration wherever practicable.

### 3. Traceability

Actions that can change data, systems, accounts, or public information should be logged in a way that allows review. Changes should be attributable to the responsible agent or human operator.

### 4. Meaningful Human Oversight

Human oversight must be practical, not merely symbolic. People should be able to review consequential actions, intervene where appropriate, and require human approval before high-impact or difficult-to-reverse operations.

## Responsibility

Delegating a task to an AI system does not remove the responsibility of the people and organizations that design, authorize, deploy, or supervise it. Responsibility should remain clear throughout the process.

## Relationship to the Axiom-Zero Framework

This is a **supplementary principle**, documented separately from the 11 Parameters. Its possible relationships to the existing framework may be studied and discussed, but such discussion must not alter the wording, number, order, or meaning of the original Parameters.

The integrity of the original work must be preserved while additional observations and principles are documented separately.
