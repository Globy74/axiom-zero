# About the Author

## Why I Write

I write because I care about how we treat one another and the world that sustains us.

These books did not arise from a desire for profit. I want to share ideas and encourage people to pause and ask themselves: What kind of future do we want to shape — for nature, the animal world and humanity?

The books do not make that decision for the reader. They invite reflection.

## Human and AI: Collaboration Without Displacement

In working on this website and my books, I use ChatGPT as a supporting tool. The experiences, questions and decisions are mine; AI helps me with wording, translation and design. To me, this collaboration shows that humans and AI can complement one another without either displacing the other.

## Experience of Two Worlds

I come from the analogue age. I have personally experienced every major stage of technological development and have worked intensively with the technologies of those periods.

I know the world before digitization as well as its transition into today's digital society. I have not merely observed this transformation; I have lived through it over several decades.

That is why I write these books. My perspective is based on direct experience of both worlds and on observing how technology, people and society influence one another.
